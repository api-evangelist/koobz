# Koobz

> Koobz is a Ventura, California additive-manufacturing company that 3D-prints footwear on demand.
> Its mono-material, circular-ready foamed-TPU shoes are printed only after a consumer orders,
> giving brands zero inventory, no minimum order quantities, and 4-6 week concept-to-market cycles.
> Koobz describes its factory as API-driven — brand storefront orders and personalization specs
> sync to the factory, which prints in Ventura and dropships to the customer — but it publishes no
> public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable API definition. Integration runs
> through its partner channel. Backed by $7.2M in funding, including Uncork Capital.

## Company

- [Koobz](https://koo.bz/): Home — the on-demand 3D-printed footwear platform and the four-step order-to-dropship flow.
- [About Us](https://koo.bz/about): Origin, thesis on breaking 18-month development cycles and overseas tooling, funding and press.
- [Technology](https://koo.bz/technology): KoobzWeave toolpath-as-textile upper pattern engine, performance validation, materials compliance (REACH, RoHS, RSL, ISO colorfastness).
- [Sustainability](https://koo.bz/sustainability): Zero-overproduction make-to-order model and mono-material architecture for EPR regimes such as California SB 707.
- [Team](https://koo.bz/team): Kuba Graczyk (CEO & Co-Founder), Micheal Hofmann (CBDO), Tomasz Cieszynski (CTO), John Dulchinos (COO).
- [Contact / Partner With Us](https://koo.bz/contact): The partner and integration intake channel.

## APIs

No public API. Koobz markets an "API sync" between a brand's storefront and the Koobz factory, but
as of 2026-07-19 there is no developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI definition, SDK,
CLI, MCP server, sandbox, or public package on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, Packagist or crates.io.
Prospective integrators go through https://koo.bz/contact.

## Notes for agents

Koobz's /robots.txt explicitly invites GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-Web
and Google-Extended, but the site sits behind a Cloudflare interactive challenge that returns a 403
interstitial to those same agents. Automated retrieval of koo.bz HTML will fail despite the stated
crawl permission.
